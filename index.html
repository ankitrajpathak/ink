Help & Documentation - INK
Using INK

Search for a brand and select its official website, or enter a URL directly. INK reads up to eight public HTML pages and shows source URLs and crawl limitations. Confirm inferred industry and audience in Settings. Add approved company context there if needed.

Write on Demand creates a source-grounded draft through the owner’s AI service. Review all facts before publishing. Export HTML or copy Markdown. Search volumes, CPC, ranking probability and private company data are not available through a public crawl.

The algorithm tracker reads official Google and Bing announcements, refreshes hourly and labels failed sources. It cannot detect undisclosed updates or guarantee rankings. Workspaces remain in this browser; Clear workspace removes them.
